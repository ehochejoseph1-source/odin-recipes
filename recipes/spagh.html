Spaghetti Recipe

The wonderful White Spaghetti and stew

This is a recipe for White Spaghetti and stew native to Nigerians

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti
  • Stew
  • A bowl of water
  • Salt

Steps

Below are the steps needed to make the wonderful delicacy

  1. Boil spagh in hot water for 15 minutes
  2. add salt while it is boiling
  3. Drain the water
  4. Serve With stew in a dish
  5. Enjoy
