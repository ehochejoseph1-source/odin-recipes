The wonderful jollof Noodles

This is a recipe for Noodles native to Nigerians

Ingredients

  • Noodles
  • Groundnut Oil
  • A bowl of water
  • Noodle seasoning
  • Cray fish
  • Pepper & Onions

Steps

Below are the steps needed to make the wonderful delicacy

  1. Boil Water in pot
  2. Put the noodles into the pot
  3. Cook for 3mins
  4. Drain the water
  5. Put it back in the pot and add a little water
  6. Add seasoning, pepper and sliced onions
  7. Add oil to your liking
  8. let it boil for 1 mins,then add your choped vegetables and crayfish
  9. Stir the food until the water almost dries
  10. Serve in a dish
  11. Enjoy
