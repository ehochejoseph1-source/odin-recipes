Yam Recipe

The wonderful Asaro yam porridge

This is a recipe for porridge yam native to Nigerians

Ingredients

  • Yam
  • Palm Oil
  • A bowl of water
  • Salt
  • Maggi
  • Cray fish
  • vegetable leaf
  • Pepper & Onions

Steps

Below are the steps needed to make the wonderful delicacy

  1. Peel and cut the yam into little squares
  2. leave the yam chunks in warm water for rinsing
  3. Boil water inside the pot
  4. wash and put the yam in the pot
  5. Boil for 20mins
  6. Add seasoning, pepper and sliced onions
  7. After 2mins add oil to your liking
  8. let it boil for 2 mins,then add your choped vegetables and crayfish
  9. Stir the food until the water becomes thick
  10. Serve in a dish
  11. Enjoy
